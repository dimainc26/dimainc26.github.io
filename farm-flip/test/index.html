FARM FLIP

Help me test Farm Flip

Farm Flip is a relaxing memory game: flip the cards, find the pairs of farm animals and hear their real sounds. It's an early version, and I'm building the next levels around what testers tell me. Before Google lets me publish it, at least 12 people have to test it for 14 days in a row. Joining takes two minutes and is free: all you need is an Android phone or tablet.

  1. Join the testers' group with the Google account you use on your Android device.
    Join the group
  2. Accept the test invitation with the same account: open the page below and tap “Become a tester”.
    Accept the invitation
    If the page says the app is not available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and reload it.
  3. Install Farm Flip from Google Play on your phone or tablet.
    Open Farm Flip on Google Play
  4. Keep it for 14 days and open it every few days for a game or two. Please don't uninstall it or leave the test before then, otherwise the 14 days might have to start again.
  5. Tell me what you think: was it too easy or too hard, were the cards and the text easy to read, how did you find the sounds and the music, did anything not work, and what would you like to see next. Write to dimazanre@gmail.com or use “Private feedback” on the app's Google Play page.

Questions

Does it cost anything? No. The test version is free and has no in-app purchases.

Does it show ads or collect data? No: no ads, no accounts, no data collected, and it works offline. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

Which devices? Android phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or later. Not available on iPhone or iPad.

What's in the test version? Four levels from 2 to 6 pairs, 12 farm animals, calm music and a pause menu. There's no timer or score yet: the game will grow with more levels and harder grids, and your feedback helps decide what comes first.

What do you see about me? Only the email address of the Google account you join the group with, used just to manage the test. I won't share it, and I'll close the group when the app is published.

Are you a developer looking for testers too? Send me your link: I'll test your app back for the full 14 days.

Thank you so much! 🐷
