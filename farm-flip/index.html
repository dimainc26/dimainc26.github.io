FARM FLIP

A relaxing memory game with farm animals

Flip the cards, find the pairs and hear each animal's real call. Farm Flip is a calm game to train your memory at your own pace: short rounds, no rush.

  • 4 levels from 2 to 6 pairs, with 12 farm animals and their real recorded sounds
  • Calm music and sound effects you can switch off; works offline on phones and tablets
  • This early version has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and collects no data

Coming soon on Google Play. The game is at an early stage and the closed test is open: your feedback will shape what comes next.
